## 2025-05-18 - Web Simulator Accessibility & Controls
**Learning:** HTML5 simulated video player components with custom controls often lack ARIA attributes (`aria-label`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-haspopup`) and visible keyboard focus states (`:focus-visible`), hindering screen reader users and keyboard navigation.
**Action:** Always complement custom UI controls with appropriate ARIA state management and distinct `:focus-visible` outline styling.
